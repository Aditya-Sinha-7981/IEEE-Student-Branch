Events

Welcome to the IEEE Student Branch at Oriental University, Indore. Established in 2023*, we are a dynamic community of future engineers, developers, and researchers driven by a passion for exploring the cutting edge of technology. Our mission aligns with IEEE’s global vision: to foster technological innovation and excellence for the benefit of humanity.


At IEEE OU SB, we go beyond the classroom. From building sophisticated full-stack web applications to pushing the boundaries of AI, machine learning, and advanced robotics development, our members are actively turning theoretical concepts into real-world solutions. We bridge the gap between academia and industry by organizing hackathons, facilitating hands-on technical workshops, and cultivating a collaborative environment where students can develop strong leadership skills and a robust professional network.
